### 1. Executive Comparison Overview

The global regulation of Artificial Intelligence is marked by three distinct philosophies:
1. **European Union:** *Horizontal, Precautionary, and Rights-Based* (Omnibus statutory regulation covering all applications with legal force).
2. **United States:** *Decentralized, Sectoral, and Innovation-Centric* (Agency-led enforcement under existing federal laws, voluntary risk frameworks, and state-level legislative innovation).
3. **India:** *Hybrid, Developmental, and Sovereignty-Focused* (Promoting domestic sovereign compute and models via the IndiaAI Mission, anchored by data protection under DPDPA 2023, and guided by MeitY administrative directives).
