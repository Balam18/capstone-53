#### Question 1
Under the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689), which of the following AI practices is classified under **Unacceptable Risk** and strictly prohibited from deployment?
- A) An AI-powered clinical decision-support tool used by radiologists for mammography screening.
- B) A social scoring system deployed by public authorities that evaluates citizens based on social behavior or personality characteristics.
- C) An automated customer service chatbot that fails to disclose its artificial nature.
- D) An enterprise predictive maintenance algorithm forecasting equipment failures in a solar farm.

## PART 2: DETAILED ANSWERS AND CONCEPTUAL EXPLANATIONS

#### Question 1: Correct Answer: B
**Explanation:**  
Under Article 5(1)(c) of the EU AI Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689), AI systems that evaluate or classify natural persons over a certain period based on their social behavior or personality characteristics (social scoring) leading to detrimental or unfavorable treatment are classified as **Unacceptable Risk** and are strictly banned. In contrast, clinical mammography tools (Option A) are High Risk (Annex III/I); chatbots without disclosure (Option C) violate transparency rules under Article 50; and equipment maintenance (Option D) is Minimal Risk.

#### Question 2: Correct Answer: C
**Explanation:**  
Article 99(3) of the EU AI Act specifies that infringements of the ban on prohibited AI practices under Article 5 are subject to administrative fines of **up to €35,000,000 or, if the offender is an undertaking, up to 7% of its total worldwide annual turnover for the preceding financial year**, whichever is higher. Option B (€20 million / 4%) corresponds to the maximum administrative penalty under GDPR Article 83(5), which is frequently confused with the EU AI Act.

#### Question 3: Correct Answer: C
**Explanation:**  
Under Section 4.2(a) of US Executive Order 14110, the reporting requirements under the Defense Production Act are triggered for any dual-use foundation model that was trained using a quantity of computing power greater than **$10^{26}$ integer or floating-point operations (FLOPs)**, or primarily using biological sequence data with a quantity of computing power greater than $10^{23}$ FLOPs.

#### Question 4: Correct Answer: B
**Explanation:**  
The NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (NIST SP 1270) structures AI risk management across four core functions: **GOVERN** (cultivating a culture of risk management and clear policies), **MAP** (contextualizing and identifying specific risks and impacts), **MEASURE** (quantitatively and qualitatively analyzing model behavior and bias), and **MANAGE** (allocating resources to mitigate identified risks). Option C represents the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), and Option D represents standard SDLC phases.

#### Question 5: Correct Answer: B
**Explanation:**  
Under Section 5 of the FTC Act, the Federal Trade Commission has established a precedent of ordering **algorithmic disgorgement** (compulsory destruction of trained algorithms, models, and embeddings) when companies (such as Everalbum, Cambridge Analytica, Weight Watchers, and Amazon) train systems on improperly acquired personal or biometric data. This prevents companies from retaining the mathematical fruits of unlawful data processing.

#### Question 6: Correct Answer: C
**Explanation:**  
Schedule 1 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 specifies that for breach in observing the obligation to implement reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach under Section 8(5), the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) may impose an administrative penalty of **up to ₹250 Crore (approx. $30 Million USD)**.

#### Question 7: Correct Answer: B
**Explanation:**  
Section 9(3) of the Indian DPDPA 2023 explicitly states: *"A Data Fiduciary shall not undertake tracking or behavioural monitoring of children or targeted advertising directed at children."* Children are defined under Section 2(f) as individuals who have not completed 18 years of age.

#### Question 8: Correct Answer: B
**Explanation:**  
The MeitY Advisories of March 1 and March 15, 2024, require that any intermediary or platform permitting the creation or hosting of synthetically generated content (audio, video, deepfakes) must ensure that such content is labeled or embedded with **permanent metadata or unique identifiers** capable of identifying the computer resource or intermediary through which the content was created or modified.

#### Question 9: Correct Answer: B
**Explanation:**  
NYC Local Law 144 of 2021 mandates that an Automated Employment Decision Tool (AEDT) cannot be used in New York City unless it has been subject to an **independent bias audit conducted no more than one year prior** to its use. The audit must calculate selection rates and impact ratios across race, ethnicity, and sex, and a summary must be published publicly on the employer's career portal.

#### Question 10: Correct Answer: B
**Explanation:**  
Diagnostic skin cancer classification software directly informs clinical triage decisions and is therefore classified as a **High-Risk AI System** under the EU AI Act (Articles 6 & Annex I) as a safety component of a medical device subject to Notified Body review under MDR 2017/745. In the US, it is regulated by the FDA as **Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)** requiring 510(k) or De Novo clearance to demonstrate clinical safety and efficacy.
